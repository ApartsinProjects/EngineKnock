# EXP-004: unsupervised cross-modal (accel<->mic) anomaly detection

**Status:** in_progress
**Date:** 2026-08-29

## Hypothesis
A normal-only learned map between accel and mic spectra captures healthy vibro-acoustic
coupling; faults break it, raising the cross-modal residual. The map is more speed-invariant
than either spectrum alone (speed moves both modalities together), so cross-modal residual
generalizes across speed where single-modality PCA collapses.

## Setup
- MaFaulDa normal + horizontal-misalignment; accel=col1 (underhang axial), mic=col7.
- Window 16 revs / 256 samp/rev -> accel & mic log-spectra (2049 each).
- Detectors (normal-only): accel_pca, mic_pca, concat_pca, xmodal_a2m, xmodal_m2a,
  xmodal_sym, xmodal_shuffle(control). Ridge in PCA-reduced space.
- Protocols: matched-speed (60% normal train) + confound-free cross-speed (train band A,
  test normal+fault both on disjoint band B). Bootstrap CIs.

## Pre-registered predictions / gates
- KEY: cross-speed mean AUC of xmodal_sym > accel_pca AND > concat_pca (agreement beats
  both single-modality and mere feature-concatenation). This is the make-or-break.
- xmodal_shuffle cross-speed AUC ~0.5 or below (destroying pairing kills the signal) ->
  confirms the residual uses real coupling, not marginal statistics.
- If xmodal does NOT beat concat_pca cross-speed, agreement adds nothing -> negative, triage.

## Headline numbers
Matched-speed AUC: accel_pca 0.856, mic_pca 0.833, concat_pca 0.926, xmodal_a2m 0.657,
  xmodal_m2a 0.945 [0.927,0.959], xmodal_sym 0.853, xmodal_shuffle 0.273.
Cross-speed mean AUC: accel_pca 0.721, concat_pca 0.702, mic_pca 0.573, xmodal_m2a 0.637,
  xmodal_sym 0.604, xmodal_a2m 0.569, xmodal_shuffle 0.528.

## Result vs pre-registered gates
- KEY gate (xmodal_sym > accel AND > concat cross-speed): FAILS. accel_pca 0.721 is best
  cross-speed; all cross-modal < accel. Cross-modal does NOT solve speed generalization.
- Shuffle control: matched-speed 0.273 (<< chance) confirms xmodal_a2m map uses real
  coupling; cross-speed shuffle 0.528 ~ chance. Control behaves correctly.
- Bright spot: xmodal_m2a 0.945 is the single best MATCHED-speed detector, but its edge over
  concat_pca 0.926 has overlapping CIs -> not clearly significant. Strong m2a/a2m asymmetry
  (0.945 vs 0.657) unexplained.

## Conclusion
Cross-modal thesis does not deliver a categorical or clearly-significant win. Best
matched-speed detector is cross-modal mic->accel (0.945) but marginal over concat; cross-speed
still owned by plain accel-PCA (0.721). Second novelty that fails to clearly beat accel-PCA.
Recommend 10,000-ft strategic reassessment with user before a third method attempt.
