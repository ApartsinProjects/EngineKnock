# EXP-007: backbone-agnosticity (PCA vs AE) of speed-conditioning

**Status:** completed (positive)
**Date:** 2026-08-29

## Hypothesis
The speed-conditioning win is not a PCA artifact: it holds under a stronger (autoencoder)
backbone too.

## Setup
- POOLED all-fault MaFaulDa (accel col1); {PCA, AE} x {uncond, cond}; matched-speed (3 seeds)
  + cross-speed (LOBO). AE = 2049-256-32-256-2049 dense, MSE, Adam, 150 epochs, CPU torch.
  Memory-light (score accumulation). Results: results/exp007_ae_results.json.

## Headline numbers (pooled)
| Backbone | uncond matched | cond matched | uncond LOBO | cond LOBO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PCA | 0.957 | 0.990 | 0.897 | 0.937 |
| AE  | 0.952 | 0.982 | 0.890 | 0.935 |
Conditioning effect: PCA +0.033 matched / +0.040 cross-speed; AE +0.030 / +0.044.

## Conclusion
Conditioning gain is backbone-agnostic (near-identical under PCA and AE). The AE alone does
NOT beat PCA alone -> conditioning, not backbone capacity, is the dominant factor. Answers the
"PCA too weak" reviewer objection.
